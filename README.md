# Project Overview
This project analyzes the Indian used car market using a dataset containing information about car manufacturers, models, prices, fuel types, transmission types, mileage, manufacturing years, and locations across India. The main objective of the project was to explore market trends, understand pricing behavior, and identify patterns that influence the resale value of used vehicles.

The project involved data cleaning, exploratory analysis, and visualization to transform raw data into meaningful insights. SQL and Excel were used for data cleaning and analysis, while Tableau was used to create interactive dashboards and visualizations.

Through this analysis, the project identifies the most popular car models, examines how factors such as manufacturing year and distance travelled affect resale prices, and highlights regional variations in the used car market across India. The dashboards and visualizations provide a clear understanding of market activity, customer preferences, and pricing trends in different regions.

# Dataset Description

The dataset used in this project contains detailed information about used car listings across different regions of India. It includes various vehicle specifications, pricing details, and market-related attributes that help analyze customer preferences, resale trends, and regional variations in the used car market.

The dataset consists of approximately 97,000 records and includes the following key attributes:

* **Manufacturer** – Name of the car manufacturer (Toyota, Honda, Suzuki, etc.)
* **Variant** – Specific model or variant of the vehicle
* **Details** – Additional information related to the vehicle
* **India_Location** – City or region where the car is listed
* **Model** – Manufacturing year of the vehicle
* **Distance_travelled** – Total distance travelled by the car in kilometers
* **Fuel_Type** – Type of fuel used (Petrol, Diesel, Electric, Hybrid, etc.)
* **Engine_Capacity** – Engine size or capacity of the vehicle
* **Transmission** – Transmission type (Manual or Automatic)
* **price_INR** – Listed resale price of the vehicle in Indian Rupees
* **Car_Age** – Age of the vehicle
* **battery_capacity_kWh** – Battery capacity for electric vehicles

This dataset was analyzed using SQL, Excel, and Tableau to identify pricing trends, popular vehicle models, mileage impact, and regional market patterns in the Indian used car industry.

# Key Insights

* Toyota Corolla was the most frequently listed used car model with **10,457 listings**, followed by Honda Civic (**5,247 listings**) and Honda City (**4,196 listings**).
* Suzuki models such as Mehran (**3,992 listings**), Alto (**3,322 listings**), and Cultus (**3,282 listings**) showed strong presence in the affordable vehicle segment.
* Average used car prices increased significantly for newer manufacturing years, rising from approximately **₹1.65 million in 2000** to over **₹10.6 million in 2023**.
* Most used cars in the dataset fall within the **50,000–120,000 km mileage range**, where resale prices vary based on brand and model.
* Major urban regions showed the highest market activity, with cities such as Delhi recording over **22,000 used car listings** in the dataset.
* Vehicles with lower mileage maintained higher resale prices, while cars with higher distance travelled showed lower average market value.
* Petrol and diesel vehicles dominated the dataset, while electric vehicle listings represented only a small portion of the market.

# Recommendations
* CARS24 should focus more on high-demand models such as Toyota Corolla, Honda Civic, and Suzuki Mehran to improve sales performance.
* The company can target metropolitan and high-activity regions for better inventory management and marketing campaigns.
* Mileage-based pricing strategies should be improved since vehicles with lower distance travelled retain higher resale value.
* Expanding electric vehicle inventory may help CARS24 capture future market demand trends.

# Conclusion
* The analysis identified Toyota, Honda, and Suzuki as the leading brands in the Indian used car market.
* Mileage, manufacturing year, and regional demand were found to be major factors affecting resale prices.
* Urban regions showed the highest used car activity and pricing trends.
* The findings can help CARS24 improve pricing strategies, inventory planning, and regional market targeting using data-driven decision making.
